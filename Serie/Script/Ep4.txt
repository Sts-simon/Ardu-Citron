The bomb doesn't need to see.
Put a GPS receiver in the nose, and the weapon knows its exact position on Earth. <break time="0.8s"/>
Satellites send signals. A computer compares the bomb's coordinates with the target's pre-programmed location, and measures the error.
Guidance turns it into commands, the fins steer, and the bomb hits the exact spot. <break time="0.8s"/>
Clouds, night, smoke, fog: none of it matters anymore. Coordinates are coordinates.
Weapons like the JDAM turn a dumb iron bomb into a precision strike using satellite navigation. <break time="0.8s"/>
Fire and forget, through any weather. <break time="0.8s"/>
But satellite signals can be jammed, spoofed, or blocked.
The guidance is a function of the coordinates. <break time="0.8s"/>
