The bomb knows nothing. No computer, no sensor, no steering, only physics. Gravity pulls it down. Its speed carries it forward. A ballistic curve. Drag slows it down. Wind pushes it off course. The pilot flies to the target and the computer calculates the release point. Unguided rockets work the same way. No steering, only a predicted impact point. But after release, nothing can be corrected. The impact is a function of the release.
